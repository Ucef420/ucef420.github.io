Research

Audio watermarking for music generation models

2025 – now
Inria Saclay, PETSCRAFT team · advised by Nicolas Anciaux

If a music model is fine-tuned on watermarked audio, does the watermark come out the other side?

I watermark training audio with neural watermarkers (AudioSeal), fine-tune a generative model on it (Stable Audio Open, MUSDB18) and test whether the mark is still detectable in what the model generates. Key finding so far: neural codec tokenization (EnCodec, DAC, Mimi) largely wipes out subtle watermarks, so codec robustness is the main bottleneck, and diffusion pipelines look like the better route. A paper is in preparation.
